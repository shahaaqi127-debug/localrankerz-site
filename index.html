FAQ

Common Questions

How is local SEO different from regular SEO?+

Regular SEO competes for visibility everywhere. Local SEO focuses specifically on your service area — showing up when someone nearby searches for what you do, primarily through Google Maps, the local map pack, and location-based search results.

How long does local SEO take to show results?+

Google Business Profile changes can show movement in weeks. Broader local SEO — citations, reviews, content — typically shows meaningful results in 60–90 days and compounds from there. Anyone promising overnight #1 rankings isn't being straight with you.

Do you guarantee rankings?+

No ethical SEO agency can guarantee a specific ranking, because Google's algorithm isn't controlled by anyone outside Google. What we do guarantee is a transparent process, real work performed every month, and reporting tied to calls and leads — not vanity metrics.

Do you work outside of my home market?+

Yes. LocalRankerz works with local service businesses across the United States, regardless of where they're based.

Do you only work on my Google Business Profile, or my website too?+

Both. Your Google Business Profile gets you found on the map — your website is what turns that visibility into rankings that hold up and pages that actually convert once someone clicks through. We handle location pages, service pages, on-page SEO, technical SEO, and off-page authority building alongside your GBP, as one connected strategy.

What does a free Google Business audit actually include?+

A full review of your categories, description, photos, reviews, citations, and how you currently stack up against the top 3 competitors ranking above you in your market — delivered with specific, prioritized fixes, not a generic checklist.
