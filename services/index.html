Services

Local SEO Services Built to Drive Calls, Not Just Rankings

Every service below exists for one reason — to get your phone ringing with customers who are already searching for what you offer. That means two things working together: your Google Business Profile getting you found on the map, and your website earning and holding the rankings once someone clicks through. We do both, as one connected strategy — not two separate line items.

Part 1 — Google Business Profile & Local Presence

Your Google Business Profile is often the very first thing a potential customer sees about your business — before your website, before anything else.

GBP

Google Business Profile Optimization

  • Primary and secondary category selection — the single highest-weighted local ranking factor
  • Business description and services written to match how customers actually search
  • Photo strategy and regular uploads to keep your profile "active" in Google's eyes
  • Weekly or biweekly GBP posts for visibility and freshness signals
  • Q&A management and messaging setup
  • Ongoing monitoring for suspensions, edits, or competitor interference
Citations

Citation Building & NAP Consistency

Google trusts businesses whose name, address, and phone number match exactly across the web. We audit and build citations across the directories that carry real authority in your industry and location, cleaning up duplicate or inconsistent listings that are actively working against you.

Reviews

Review Generation & Management

  • A simple, repeatable system for asking happy customers for reviews
  • Keyword-rich, personalized responses to every review — including negative ones
  • Monitoring for review velocity and authenticity signals Google now weighs heavily
Part 2 — Website & Technical Local SEO

Ranking on the map gets you seen. Ranking on the website gets you found for every service and every area you cover — and gives people a reason to trust you once they click through. This is where we build the site itself into a local search asset instead of a digital business card.

Website

Location Pages

If you serve more than one city, town, or service area, a single homepage can't rank for all of them — Google needs a dedicated, genuinely useful page for each location you want to show up in. We build location pages with real local detail instead of the thin, duplicated "city + keyword" pages Google actively penalizes.

Website

Service Pages

A dedicated page for every core service you offer, written around how customers actually search for it — not generic descriptions copied from a template. Each page is built to answer the exact question a searcher has and to convert once they land on it.

Website

On-Page SEO

  • Title tags, meta descriptions, and header structure built around real search intent
  • Internal linking strategy so your strongest pages support the ones you most need to rank
  • Content structure and keyword targeting aligned to how customers actually phrase searches
  • Image optimization, alt text, and page structure cleanup
Website

Technical SEO

  • Site speed and Core Web Vitals — a direct conversion factor, not just a ranking one
  • Mobile performance and usability — most local searches happen on mobile
  • Crawlability, indexing, and XML sitemap health
  • Schema markup so both Google and AI answer engines can accurately understand your business
  • Fixing broken links, duplicate content, and redirect chains quietly capping your rankings
Website

Off-Page SEO & Local Authority Building

Rankings aren't decided by your website alone — Google also weighs the trust and authority signals pointing to it from elsewhere. We build local backlinks and authority signals the right way: local business associations, relevant local press and directories, and genuine industry sources — never spam link networks that put your site at risk.
